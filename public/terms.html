Article 05

52-Point Inspection & 100% Reclean Guarantee

task_alt Quality Assurance

We deliver results that meet stringent hospitality standards. We do not settle for surface wipe-downs. Every clean is measured against our published 52-Point Cleanliness Standard.

The 24-Hour Notice Window

If any specified area from our checklist was overlooked, clients must notify us via text, call, or email within 24 hours of appointment completion. Including a quick smartphone photo helps us dispatch the exact right tools.

Immediate Correction Dispatch

We will dispatch a team lead back to your residence within 24 business hours at $0 additional charge. If you remain unsatisfied after our complimentary reclean, your booking escrow will be promptly refunded.

Article 06

Housekeeper Safety, Eco-Botanical Supplies & Pet Policy

eco Non-Toxic Products

The physical health of your household and our cleaning professionals is central to our values.

spa
Commercial Botanical Solutions: We bring our own EPA-registered, plant-based sanitizers, microfiber cloths, HEPA-sealed vacuums, and pH-neutral floor detergents. Clients who prefer their own specific specialty hardwood oil or natural stone polish may provide them at the start of service.

Article 07

Property Access, Entry Codes & Liability Limits

key Security Protocols

Approximately 70% of our clients are away at work during service. We treat key codes and property access with bank-grade encryption protocols:

  • Encrypted Digital Codes: Smart lock access codes, garage keypad numbers, or lockbox combinations are stored in an encrypted vault accessible only to the dispatched lead cleaner 15 minutes before the arrival window.
  • Comprehensive Insurance & Bonding: All cleaning crew members are fully background-checked, bonded, and backed by a $2,000,000 general liability policy through nationwide commercial carriers.
  • Fragile & Irreplaceable Heirlooms: Clients are requested to inform dispatch in advance of irreplaceable antiques, artwork valued over $1,000, or fragile family heirlooms so they can be explicitly marked 'do not disturb' on your residence work order.
Article 08

Dispute Escalation & Central PA Local Arbitration

balance Pennsylvania Law

In the improbable event of an unresolved dispute, both parties commit to an informal 14-day direct resolution dialogue with our Managing Dispatch Director.

If a consensus cannot be reached, claims shall be resolved under binding arbitration pursuant to the rules of the American Arbitration Association, seated locally within Clearfield County or Jefferson County, Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. Both customer and service provider waive rights to participate in class-action litigation to foster swift, low-cost local justice.
