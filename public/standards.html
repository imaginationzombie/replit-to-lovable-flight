Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our scheduling buffers, guarantees, payments, and cleaner ethics.

We reject arbitrary on-site bait-and-switch pricing. Our transparent Instant Quote Estimator bases your clean on your exact bedroom and bathroom counts, estimated home square footage, and chosen tier (Standard Refresh, Deep Detail, or Move-In Turnover). Any specialized add-ons (inside the oven, inside the refrigerator, basement steps) are priced as clear flat rates with no surprise travel surcharges anywhere in our DuBois or Brookville service radius.

Zero charges occur at the moment you book online. A temporary pre-authorization hold is placed on your credit or debit card 24 hours prior to appointment dispatch to verify funds. We only capture and finalize payment once your assigned housekeeper has completed the checklist and you have had an opportunity to review the home.

We understand rural PA schedules and unexpected weather. You can reschedule or cancel with zero penalty up to 24 hours before your scheduled arrival window directly via your customer portal or by calling direct dispatch at (814) 952-1964. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice incur a modest $45 dispatch compensation fee paid directly to your scheduled housekeeper.

If you have custom wood oil or specialty marble polish you prefer us to apply, simply set it on the counter with instructions.

You are backed by our absolute 24-Hour 100% Reclean Guarantee. If a fixture was missed or a surface was not completed to our published protocol, call or text us within 24 hours of service.
