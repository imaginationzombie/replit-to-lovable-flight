- Marketing uses public/*.html ("/" redirects to /index.html); interactive features use React routes and shared tokens. Maintain public/sitemap.xml for inspected public pages only, excluding private/demo/checkout pages; omit lastmod without page-specific dates. Why: preserves the migrated design without advertising internal demos.
- Homepage phone styles stay inline; its location module selects the nearest area with browser permission and syncs the estimator. Why: keep coordinates on-device and manual choices intact.
- Cleaning levels use shared quote fees persisted and copied to repeat bookings; static estimators mirror them. Why: charges match the chosen level.
- AI uses server-only helpers behind server functions. Why: keeps keys private.
- Accounts: roles live in user_roles (admin/cleaner/customer, checked via has_role); a signup trigger creates the profile + customer role. Signed-in pages live under src/routes/_authenticated and read data with the browser client under RLS. Why: no privilege escalation, and access is enforced by the database.
- Staff calendars use shared dnd-kit and schedule_booking with permissions, conflicts and versions; creator-owned pending phone requests allow date/window moves without confirmation or assignment. Customer AppointmentCalendar requests windows, not live availability. Why: no private schedule leaks or false confirmations.
- The availability creator edits only the signed-in cleaner's existing cleaner_settings hours, days off and accepting status; shared validation also applies to the profile editor. Why: availability is consistent across jobs and admin views without replacing profile or rate data.
- Customer account and status pages share an explicitly owner-filtered bookings query under RLS, including verified exact-email guest bookings and periodic refresh; payment history and balance buttons reuse it with token-authorized PayPal checkout, keeping customer charges distinct from cleaner wages. Why: multi-role customers still see only their own visits and scheduling changes appear automatically.
- Booking mutations refresh all cached views; staff dashboard date/cleaner filters are URL-backed and RLS-scoped, with shared calendar filters. Lists poll every 15 seconds. Why: synchronized schedules without expanding cleaner access to other customers.
- Booking/payment notices use persisted recipients and event keys after confirmation/verified capture; failures never fail payment or imply wage settlement. Staff phone requests store created_by_staff, never staff customer ownership; role-checked estimates email saved recipients and token checkout authorizes saved totals. Why: no spoofing, duplicate notices or upfront charges.
- Owner booking alerts use a fixed template recipient and one idempotency key per confirmed visit, independently of customer email delivery. Why: new appointments reach the owner without duplicate alerts or customer-controlled recipients.
- Waiting-list RPCs expose only appointment scope and location area, never customer contact or payment data, and atomically claim unassigned confirmed visits with cleaner-role, availability and conflict checks. Why: employees fill their schedules without accessing unassigned customers' private data or double-booking.
- Appointment work records use cleaner-owned authenticated RPCs; submission snapshots hourly earnings and locks edits, while admin-only review records approval and externally completed payments. Why: timesheets cannot self-approve and customer PayPal payments remain separate from employee payouts.
- Keep the project on Vite 7 and use exact legacy entities subpath aliases only, never a broad entities alias. Why: newer HTML parsers need their own entities version, and forcing the legacy package stalls dependency optimization and leaves portals blank.
- Timesheet approval uses an admin-authorized approve-and-pay server function, validates a receiving email before approval, and reserves one permanent PayPal operation per timesheet; pending receipts reconcile while either portal is open, uncertain sends never retry and tests never settle live wages. Why: approval initiates payment without duplicate transfers or false settlement.
- The admin PayPal dashboard reuses existing payout controls and classifies settlement only from live successful payouts with paid work receipts, separating manual records, tests and uncertain transfers. Why: summary totals cannot imply money was delivered without verified receipts.
- Customer tips are separate post-cleaning PayPal captures and separate one-shot cleaner payouts; booking access tokens authorize customer actions and only verified completed receipts credit tips. Why: voluntary tips never alter recurring charges or frozen timesheet wages.
- Hiring review enables cleaner roles only through the admin-checked review_application RPC; applicants cannot change their own status. Why: applying cannot grant staff access.
- Messaging uses participant-scoped threads and authenticated RPC sends with server-derived sender names; booking participation follows the current cleaner assignment. Why: users cannot spoof messages or read another customer’s conversations.
- The panel links to /my-page for owner-scoped portfolio edits, refreshing owner/public queries. Public pages use opt-in fields, expiring photos and shared quotes; requested_cleaner_id validates role, publication, availability and conflicts. Why: consistent pages without exposing schedules or double-booking.
- Admin date blocks live separately from cleaner settings, with admin-only mutation and a booking-write validation trigger under the shared cleaner lock. Why: cleaners cannot erase admin restrictions, all assignment paths enforce them, and active visits must be rescheduled before blocking.
- Disputes and completed-visit reviews use owner-checked RPCs; only admins resolve reports, and public reviews expose chosen display names, never booking/contact IDs. Why: customer claims cannot change payment receipts or leak private data.
- Time clock entries and location pings are written only through clock_in/record_location/clock_out RPCs (staff, assigned confirmed visits; admins may clock general shifts); customers read live location only via booking_cleaner_location while the assigned cleaner is clocked in. Why: location is never exposed outside an active visit.
